7 · Decision ledger

Confirmed, proposed, open

Kept apart on purpose, as your product document asks. Only the first column is settled.

Confirmed
  • Four company types, one type per company. Driver and Master Admin are actors, not types.
  • Connection required before any invitation.
  • Transporter bids, wins, accepts, then allocates. Own fleet or subcontract to Fleet Owners.
  • Five bid attempts per transporter, history visible to that transporter only.
  • Competitor names and amounts stay hidden. A transporter may see its own rank.
  • Bid calculator: base rate plus percentage or fixed margin, system computes the final bid.
  • Lowest price does not automatically win. The Customer chooses.
  • RFQ = requirement, Bid = offer, Contract = awarded business, Trip = execution.
  • Master Admin is a user with no company, created once by a seed script from environment variables.
Proposed by me
  • One RFQ table with a kind column (rfq, ad_hoc), one bids table, one award flow.
  • Invitations are a snapshot of connected transporters chosen at publish time.
  • Award needs transporter acceptance within 24 hours, or the Customer awards again.
  • The Load is its own table between Contract and Trip, and is what Fleet Owners see.
  • Rank and the closing clock run on polling and an in-process timer first. WebSockets later.
  • Drivers start as company records with no login.
  • Build order: shell and approval, roles and team, notifications, RFQ and Ad Hoc with contracts, fleet and drivers, loads and trips, earnings and reports.Earlier I proposed that brokers and fleet owners also bid. Withdrawn after reading the product document.
Open
  • Customer-side Ad Hoc creation and how it differs from an RFQ.
  • Public versus invitation-only bidding.
  • Broker's role in RFQ and Ad Hoc. Landing page says brokers bid; document says they manage loads.
  • Role names: root_admin and co-admin in code versus Root Admin and Master Admin in the document.
  • Contract cancellation and modification rules.
  • Payment, invoicing and settlement.
  • Driver login: mobile app or company account.
  • Subscription pricing, disputes and penalties, document verification depth.
