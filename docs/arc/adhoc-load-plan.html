How an Ad Hoc load is created and bid on

The Figma file draws an eight-screen Create Load wizard. This page reads those screens, maps them to the Ad Hoc flow Enroute actually needs, and lets you mark what to keep, change or drop before anyone builds it.

How to mark this page

Every screen, step, field, fix and decision has a control. Click to mark it, type a note under it, then press Copy my marks and paste the text back to me. Marks are saved in this browser only.

Keep / Agree / Yes Change / Later Merge · Required field Drop / No / Hidden field Recommended = my pick, change it freely

Vocabulary used here, from the product document: an Ad Hoc load is one load on one date raised by a Customer. A bid is a transporter's offer on it. An award is the Customer picking one bid. The awarded bid becomes a contract for that load, then a trip. "Create a bid" in everyday talk means the Customer creating the load that transporters bid on.

2 · Daily, Contract, Ad Hoc

Where Ad Hoc sits among the three load modes

Step 1 of the design offers three modes. The product document defines Contract (an RFQ: a lane or volume over a period) and Ad Hoc (one load, one date). It never defines Daily. The Daily column is my assumption and is decision D1 below.

Because the mode decides which wizard opens, the mode chip does not belong inside the wizard. The Customer picks "New Ad Hoc" first and never sees the Daily or Contract chips again.

RULES

Rules the diagram relies on

  • Invitations are a snapshot taken at publish. Adding a transporter later is a new invitation with its own timestamp.
  • Window must close before pickup. Validation at publish, and the clock job closes the window on time.
  • Five attempts per transporter, every attempt stored. Rank recomputed on each attempt, ties broken by earlier time.
  • Own rank only. A transporter sees L1, L2, L3 for itself, never a rival's name or amount. The Customer sees everything.
  • Lowest does not auto-win. The Customer awards any bid and may record a reason.
  • Acceptance within 24 hours (proposed). Silence or decline sends the load back to Closed for a second award.
  • Cancel rules are open. Before award: free. After award: needs a decision (D6 note).
